Where records are made

The short version: The world's biggest presser sits in a small town near Prague: GZ Media, which pressed more than 55 million records in 2021 and runs plants on two continents. North America went from about 15 plants in 2014 to a projected 43 in the US alone by 2022, while Japan still had at least one fully functioning plant in 2010 and Brazil had a second plant on the way in 2016.

Where is the biggest pressing plant in the world?#

In Loděnice, a small town near Prague, where the state-run Gramofonové závody pressed its first record in 1951.88 Today it is GZ Media, which Radio Prague International called the world's biggest vinyl producer in 2022.88

In the mid-1990s the plant made about 300,000 records a year.88 In 2021 it made more than 55 million, and by 2022 it was turning out almost 200,000 a day.88 That is, in one day, two-thirds of what it once made in a year.

GZ Media had grown to eight plants by 2022, including three in North America (Burlington, Ontario; Memphis; Nashville) and one in France (reported, 2022).88

How many plants are there in North America?#

Far more than a decade ago. North America had about 15 plants in 2014; the Vinyl Record Manufacturers Association projected 43 US plants by summer 2022, plus nine in Canada and three in Mexico.177 Those are trade-body estimates, made in 2022 in the middle of a backlog, when lead times "routinely" ran 9-12 months.177

A few US plants publish their prices. National Audio Company in Springfield, Missouri,174 posts a full 2026 price card;173 Gotta Groove Records in Cleveland posted its 180 gram card in 2024.176 What those cards say about the cost of a run is in what a record costs.

Mexico's plants include Vantiva's in Guadalajara, which the company describes as a 273,000-square-foot facility with a future capacity of more than 17 million units a year on 40 presses.193 That capacity is Vantiva's own projection.

Is there still a pressing plant in Japan?#

Yes, at least one, and for a stretch it was described as the only one in Asia. In the late 2000s Time Out Tokyo called Toyo Kasei's Suehiro factory in Yokohama "the only active record pressing plant in Asia."194 In 2010 the Japan Times called Toyo Kasei the owner of "Japan's last fully functioning pressing plant."195 It handles cutting, pressing and label printing in-house, so a record can go from cut to labeled under one roof.194

Those descriptions are from 2010 and the late 2000s, and more presses have come since. In June 2017 Sony Music Entertainment (Japan) said it would resume vinyl production for the first time since 1989, with a press installed at a factory in central Japan; the Japan Times then described Toyo Kasei as "believed to be Japan's only remaining record maker."196 Japan made 799,000 records in 2016, up from 105,000 in 2010, by the Recording Industry Association of Japan's count.196 The label P-Vine finished its own plant, Vinyl Goes Around Pressing, in 2024, and by 2025 Brutus magazine was listing Toyo Kasei and Sony as the other Japanese pressing factories.197 Nobody has published a current count of Japan's plants.

How big is vinyl in the UK?#

The UK bought about 7.6 million vinyl LPs in 2025, up 13.3%, the 18th straight year of growth.29 Its top vinyl album of the year, Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl, sold over 147,000 vinyl copies, the most for any release in a calendar year since the Official Charts Company launched in 1994.29

On the making side, the UK is home to Evolution Music, which presses records from a sugar-and-starch bioplastic rather than PVC.191

Are records made in Germany?#

Germany is where optimal media189 launched BioVinyl with PlastChem in July 2023, a PVC whose petroleum feedstock is replaced with recycled used cooking oil or industrial waste gases.190 The details are in vinyl and the planet.

As a market, Germany leans physical more than most. German music retail turned over EUR 2.42 billion in 2025, up 2.3%; physical formats were 14.2% of revenue; vinyl grew 2.8%; and CDs were still 44.2% of the physical market.198 The industry body BVMI gives no separate euro figure for vinyl.

Are records made in the Netherlands?#

Green Vinyl Records, in the Eindhoven region, came out of the CD and DVD world as a Symcon Group project and injection-molds records from its own non-PVC compounds, printing straight onto the disc instead of using paper labels.192 It claims 93% less energy than steam pressing, by its own measurement.192

Who presses records in Latin America?#

In 2016, Brazil's Polysom in Rio de Janeiro was described as Latin America's only pressing plant, at about 40,000 records a month.199 That January, The Vinyl Factory reported a second Brazilian plant on the way: Vinyl Brazil, in São Paulo on the former Continental Records site, founded by Michel Nath and set to open at about 140,000 records a month.199 That number was a projection, not confirmed output.199

By 2022 the VRMA counted three plants in Mexico.177

Argentina was set to get its first new pressing plant in 2016: Laser Disc, due to start manufacturing at the end of March with two presses, aiming for 40,000 records a month once in full swing.200 That too was a plan, not confirmed output.200

Why does it matter where a record is pressed?#

For a buyer it mostly stays invisible; plant marks in the runout groove are covered in reading the runout. For an artist, location sets shipping costs and, in a crunch year like 2022, the queue you join. The pricing is in what a record costs.
