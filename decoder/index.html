Runout decoder: what the etchings in the dead wax mean

The smooth band between the last song and the label is called the runout, or dead wax. Cutting engineers and plants scratch and stamp codes there. Type what you see.

Runout decoder

130 markings from the dead wax

Search the letters, numbers, initials and symbols scratched or stamped between the last song and the label. Every row links to its source; plants and engineers varied their habits over the years, so a marking is a clue, not proof.
