The short version: Pressing costs a few dollars a record at 1,000 copies and $10 or more at 100, because setup costs land on every run whatever its size. Sell the result for $24 in an indie shop and, in one label's breakdown, the label and the artist each keep about $3.19. US vinyl revenue passed $1 billion in 2025 for the first time since 1983.

How much does it cost to press 300 records?#

On one US plant's published 2026 price card, a 300-copy run of black 140 gram LPs in a full-color jacket comes to $5.56 a record.173 That is National Audio Company in Springfield, Missouri,174 and its "combo" package, effective June 1, 2026, prices every quantity in the open.173 These are official list prices, not negotiated quotes, and the per-unit figure is what you pay for each finished, shrink-wrapped record:

Run size 140 g black 180 g black 180 g basic color
100 $11.69 $11.82 $13.29
300 $5.56 $5.71 $6.90
500 $4.28 $4.41 $5.55
1,000 $3.35 $3.58 $4.68

National Audio Company 12-inch combo package, per unit, official list price, June 2026.173

Going from 140 to 180 grams adds only 13 to 23 cents a record at these quantities, so the heavier pressing is not where the money goes.173 Color costs more: about $1.10 to $1.47 a record over 180 g black.173 The real swing is quantity: the 100-copy record costs about three and a half times the 1,000-copy record.173

Whether 180 grams sounds better is a separate, argued question; the card only shows it is cheap to choose.

What is in a combo price, and what costs extra?#

A combo package is a plant's bundled price for the whole object, not only the vinyl.173 On the National Audio Company card it covers the lacquers (the lacquer-coated aluminum discs the grooves are first cut into),175 two-step plating, up to 21 minutes 59 seconds per side, center labels, poly-lined dust sleeves and a full-color jacket with a spine, shrink-wrapped.173 The extras are priced line by line, as of 2026:173

Add-on Price (2026, official)
Test pressings (up to 10) $80
Longer side, 22:00-25:00 $65 per side
Printed inner sleeve $0.52-$0.98 per unit
Old-style tip-on jacket $2.44-$3.46 per unit
Rush, 4-5 weeks $1.24 per record
Rush, 3-4 weeks $2.47 per record

A test pressing is a handful of early copies, made from the same material with the same grooves, that the artist approves before the full run goes through.173175 A tip-on jacket is the "old style" construction, with the print on a separate sheet of paper wrapped and glued over a thick corrugated core.173175

Other plants split the bill differently. Gotta Groove Records in Cleveland prices records and jackets separately on its 2024 card.176 Its 180 gram "Step 1" price (records only) includes lacquers, plating, five test pressings, black ink on white paper labels and plain white inner sleeves:176

Run size 180 g black, total 180 g natural, total Standard jacket, total
100 $1,660 $2,015 $550
300 $2,395 $3,055 $575
500 $2,855 $3,900 $595
1,000 $4,605 $6,610 $800

Gotta Groove Records 2024 price card, official list price.176

Divide the totals out and 300 black LPs with standard jackets come to about $9.90 a record, and 1,000 to about $5.41, before shipping and sleeves; the plant itself doesn't quote a per-record price.176 The same card adds shrinkwrap at $0.35 a unit, extra test pressings at $5 each, asks a 50% deposit before work begins, and leaves shipping to the buyer, with a rough estimate of $200 minimum for 100 LPs and $350-600 for 1,000.176 The two cards are from different years and bundle different things, so they are not a like-for-like comparison.

Why does 100 cost so much more per record than 1,000?#

Because a run of 100 and a run of 1,000 share the same expensive first steps. Somebody cuts lacquers, a plating shop grows metal parts from them, test pressings get made and checked, labels get printed. Those costs do not shrink with the order, so they divide over fewer records. Gotta Groove's card makes the lacquer cost visible: deduct $400 if you supply your own.176

Plating comes in steps. Gotta Groove includes 2-step plating for orders up to 499 records and 3-step at 500 and up.176 The third step adds a metal "father" that can make more "mothers", and mothers are what stampers (the metal negatives that press the grooves into the plastic) are made from.175

What is the minimum order?#

Minimums are set plant by plant, and the price card is where to look. National Audio Company's combo packages start at 100 units, and its budget package goes down to 50 (2026).173 Gotta Groove's 2024 180 gram card starts its price table at 100.176 Below that, people usually turn to lathe cutting, a different process covered in how a record is cut.

How long does pressing take?#

It depends on the year. In 2018 plants could deliver in four to six weeks.177 By mid-2022 orders across the industry "routinely" took 9-12 months, and one new plant was quoting five to six months.177 Its owner said the price of PVC had gone up three times since the plant opened in 2020.177

Capacity grew to meet it. North America had about 15 plants in 2014; the Vinyl Record Manufacturers Association projected 43 in the US by summer 2022, plus nine in Canada and three in Mexico.177

By 2026, National Audio Company lists six to eight weeks as its standard turnaround, with paid rush tiers at four to five and three to four weeks.173 That is one plant's official card, not an industry average.

The backlog was already easing by May 2023, helped by the new plants, and one plant owner said most supply-chain problems had cleared.86 Gotta Groove Records' planning page puts most orders at about two to three months and advises planning for at least three.178

Why does a new LP cost $30 or more?#

In dollars, records used to cost far less. Single-disc LP list prices in the 1970s and '80s ran from $5.98 to $8.98; an $8.98 record in 1982 works out to about $26.63 in 2022 dollars.177 In 2022 a new major-artist record could cost $45; a record-store owner said buyers balked at $40, and the head of the VRMA said demand would hold as long as buyers would pay $30 for a black record.177

Measured against pay, the picture flips for recorded music overall. Matt Brennan of the University of Glasgow found that recorded music cost about 4.83% of an average US weekly salary in 1977, the vinyl peak, against about 1.22% in 2013.179

Who gets what from a $24 LP?#

In one label's published numbers, the artist and the label each take home about $3.19 from a $24 record sold in an independent shop.180 Colemine Records made the breakdown public in 2024, with the caveat that the figures apply to that one release:180

Who Share of $24 Approx. %
Retailer (pays card fees and shipping out of this) $8.49 35%
Manufacturer $6.03 25%
Distributor $3.10 13%
Label $3.19 13%
Artist $3.19 13%

One release, label's own figures, reported 2024.180

The label and artist halves come from about $6.37 left over, split evenly.180 A different contract, a bigger run or a direct sale at a show would change every line, so read it as one example, not a rule. The money in live shows works on different math; tickets.cold.fyi covers that side.

Why are there so many versions of the same album?#

A variant is the same album in a different color, cover or retailer-exclusive edition. Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl, released October 3, 2025, sold 1.2 million vinyl copies on its first day, beating the previous single-week vinyl record of 859,000 set by The Tortured Poets Department.181 Its first-week report listed Target CDs, a Target vinyl, several vinyl "collections", a standard LP and a cassette.181 By March 2026 Luminate counted 27 physical editions.31

The same report counted 4.002 million equivalent album units (sales plus streams and track downloads) and 3,479,500 pure album sales (purchases only) in week one.181

Whether a run of editions rewards superfans or squeezes them is argued both ways. The average number of variants among Billboard 200 top-10 albums went from 3.3 a week in 2019 to 8.9 a week in 2023.182 Industry executives describe variants as a way for fans to show loyalty, support a favorite shop, or find out-of-print catalog again.182 On the other side, in a 2024 Northeastern University piece, music professor Andrew Mall called staggered preorders that make fans pay shipping several times "a wasteful aspect," while also arguing that records are mostly kept rather than thrown away.183

How big is vinyl now?#

In the US, the RIAA's 2025 year-end report put recorded-music revenue at a record $11.5 billion, with vinyl revenue passing $1 billion for the first time since 1983, up 9.3%, the 19th straight year of growth.30 Vinyl was 75% of US physical revenue; CDs brought in $312 million, down nearly 8%.184 In the first half of 2026 the RIAA reported about $6 billion in total revenue, physical up 25.9%, vinyl up 17.7%, and CDs up 58.6%.32

The two main US counters give different unit totals:

2025 US vinyl units Count As reported
RIAA 46.8 million RIAA year-end figures30
Luminate 47.8 million Vinyl records sold31

The RIAA also counted 29.5 million CDs in 2025.30 Luminate put Q4 2025 vinyl up 13.1% on Q4 2024.31

Who bought them: The Life of a Showgirl sold 1.6 million US vinyl copies in 2025, over 3% of all vinyl units; Sabrina Carpenter's Man's Best Friend sold 292,000 and Kendrick Lamar's GNX 279,000.185 Roughly half of vinyl buyers were nonwhite, and about two in five records were sold at independent stores.185

How big is vinyl outside the US?#

The UK bought about 7.6 million vinyl LPs in 2025, up 13.3% after 9.1% growth in 2024, its 18th straight year of growth; streaming was still 89.3% of album consumption.29 The Life of a Showgirl topped UK vinyl too, with over 147,000 copies, the most for any release in a calendar year since the Official Charts Company launched in 1994, and 126,000 of them in its first week.29186 Germany's numbers, and where all these records are pressed, are in where records are made.

Quick answers

How much does it cost to press 300 vinyl records?
On National Audio Company's June 2026 price card, 300 black 140 gram LPs with a full-color jacket cost $5.56 each, or about $1,668. Gotta Groove's 2024 card works out to about $9.90 a record for 300 180 gram LPs with standard jackets, before shipping.
How long does it take to get vinyl pressed in 2026?
One US plant, National Audio Company, lists 6-8 weeks as its standard turnaround on its 2026 card, with paid rush tiers down to 3-4 weeks. In 2022, industry-wide waits of 9-12 months were reported.
How much does an artist make from a vinyl record sale?
In one label's published breakdown of a $24 indie-store LP (2024), the artist and label each got about $3.19. The retailer kept about $8.49, the manufacturer about $6.03 and the distributor about $3.10.
What was the best-selling vinyl album of 2025?
Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl: 1.6 million US vinyl copies, and over 147,000 in the UK, the most for any release in a calendar year there since 1994.
